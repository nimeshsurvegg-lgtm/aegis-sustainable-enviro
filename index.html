Official Mandates

Swachh Directives

Stay informed about government policies and directives driving our mission.

Mission LiFE (Lifestyle for Environment)

Launched by the Hon'ble Prime Minister, Mission LiFE aims to nudge individuals and communities to practice a lifestyle that is synchronous with nature and does not harm it. It focuses on replacing the 'use-and-dispose' economy with a circular economy.

Solid Waste Management Rules 2016

Mandates segregation of waste at source into three streams: Biodegradable (wet), Non-biodegradable (dry), and Domestic Hazardous waste (diapers, batteries). All citizens are required to hand over segregated waste to authorized rag-pickers or waste collectors.

Swachh Bharat Mission Urban 2.0

Aims to make all cities 'Garbage Free' and ensure grey and black water management in all cities. Focuses on source segregation of solid waste, utilizing the principles of 3Rs (Reduce, Reuse, Recycle).

E-Waste (Management) Rules, 2022

Enforces Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) to ensure IT equipment, consumer electronics, and solar PV modules are responsibly recycled. Mandates citizens to route e-waste strictly through authorized recyclers to prevent hazardous soil contamination.

Plastic Waste Management Amendment, 2021

Prohibits the manufacture, import, stocking, distribution, sale, and use of identified single-use plastic items, which have low utility and high littering potential (e.g., plastic cutlery, earbuds, plastic sticks for balloons).

National Clean Air Programme (NCAP)

A long-term, time-bound, national-level strategy to tackle air pollution problems across India in a comprehensive manner with targets to achieve a 20% to 30% reduction in Particulate Matter (PM2.5 and PM10) concentrations.
